# Novara Media HQ walkthrough

A first-person walkthrough of Novara Media's new office and studios at Fitzroy House, Abbot Street, Hackney. It runs in the browser on desktop and phone.

- Desktop: W A S D to walk, mouse to look, Shift to go faster. Click the view to take control, Esc to let go.
- Phone: left thumb walks, right thumb looks. Landscape works best.
- The plan in the corner jumps you anywhere you tap. "Go to" lists every room.

The model is built in Blender from zector architects' drawings (095/101 rev E and related sheets). Set design and furniture are indicative.

## Files

- `index.html` is the whole app. It loads three.js 0.160 from jsDelivr.
- `fh3.glb` is the model, exported from `FH3_GA_plan_3rd_floor V1.blend`.

To update the model, export a new `fh3.glb` and replace the file.
